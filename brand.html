Shubho Chithi Brand Guide

Logo, colours and fonts for the website, the invitation cards, and Facebook, Instagram and TikTok posts.

Logo

The mark is a jharokha arch (the shape of Mughal and Bengali palace windows) holding an envelope sealed with a golden heart. It says "wedding letter" in one shape.

✓ Do
  • Leave space around the logo at least the size of the gold seal.
  • Use the dark-background version on maroon or photos.
  • Use the mark alone when space is small.
✗ Don't
  • Stretch, rotate or recolour the mark.
  • Put the maroon logo on a busy photo.
  • Retype the wordmark in a different font.

Social post recipe

Every post: ivory or maroon background → one Lipighor calligraphy headline → Hind Siliguri for details → gold divider or ornament → logo in a corner → shubhochithi.com.
Reels: 9:16, the phone-in-hand reveal in the first 2 seconds, a hook line in Hind Siliguri Bold on a maroon strip, and the logo at the end.
