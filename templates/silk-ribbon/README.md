# Silk Ribbon — modern blush & champagne, untie the bow

A gatefold card tied with a satin ribbon and bow. The guest pulls a ribbon tail (or taps the bow): the bow loosens, the ribbons slide away and the two card doors open onto the couple photo.
Then: an arch photo, names, families, events with a ribbon corner, round countdown, a "Save the date + reminder" button, RSVP and thank you.

Per client: edit `data.js` (same format as every design; set `photo` to the couple's picture).
