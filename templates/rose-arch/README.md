# Rose Arch — trending 2027 style

Inspired by the viral "2027 wedding invite" format:
- **Opening:** a dusty-rose **embossed envelope** (carved-paper leaves made with an SVG lighting filter). The guest taps the wax seal, the flap lifts and the envelope slides away.
- **Hero:** the couple's photo appears through **mist inside a rose arch**, with names, parents and **falling rose petals**.
- **Hanging date scroll** on a gold rod with a swinging tassel, then the countdown and **Add to calendar** (with reminders).
- **Wedding Festivities carousel:** swipeable 3D cards, one per event. Each card's colour and icon come from the event name (Holud yellow, Mehendi green, Akdh emerald, Biye red, Walima gold), and it shows a **dress code** swatch plus map and save buttons.
- RSVP, contacts, music and share, like every design.

Per client: edit `data.js`. Optional per event: `dressCode`, `color` (CSS colour or gradient) and `icon` (an emoji).
