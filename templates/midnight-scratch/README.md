# Midnight Scratch — navy & gold, scratch to reveal

Luxury dark design with a Jamdani-style diamond pattern, twinkling stars and floating lanterns.
The guest scratches gold foil with a finger to reveal the couple's names and date, with a sparkle burst and a short vibration.
A "Can't scratch? Tap here" link is there for anyone who has trouble scratching.
Then a moonlit page opens: names, families under arches, the events on a timeline, ring countdown, photos, RSVP and thank you.

**Per client:** edit only `data.js` (same format as the other designs).

The link generator, RSVP sheet setup and publishing steps are the same as in `../royal-envelope/README.md`.
