# Emerald Scroll — Islamic emerald & gold, pull to unroll

A rolled scroll with gold rollers and an emerald wax seal on a red ribbon. The guest pulls the bottom roller down, or taps the seal, and the parchment unrolls.
Inside: a Mughal arch with Bismillah, Surah Ar-Rum 30:21 with its translation, names, families, events in arch cards with an 8-point-star date badge, star countdown, RSVP, contacts and the wedding dua (Barakallahu lakuma…).
Made for Akdh, Nikah and Walima.

**Per client:** edit only `data.js` (same format as the other designs).
Optional: `showVerse: false` hides the Quran verse.

The link generator, RSVP sheet setup and publishing steps are the same as in `../royal-envelope/README.md`.
